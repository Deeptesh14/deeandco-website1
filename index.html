The Firm

Capital is abundant.
Trust is scarce.

"We do not pursue volume.
We pursue the right work."

Dee & Co is a private advisory practice serving a select group of founders and investors navigating complex capital decisions. We operate with discretion across private markets, growth equity, and strategic transitions.

Our work sits at the convergence of capital advisory, transaction origination, and long-horizon relationship management. We do not maintain a broad client roster — we maintain a concentrated portfolio of trusted relationships, each served with the depth of a long-term partner rather than the efficiency of a service provider.

Engagements are accepted by introduction, or upon our own initiative when we identify an opportunity of compelling merit.

How We Work

A deliberate process,
not a pipeline.

I.
Understand

We begin with deep listening — your business, your capital needs, your timeline, and the specific outcome you are working toward.

II.
Evaluate

We assess readiness, refine positioning, and identify the right investor profiles — aligning strategy before any outreach begins.

III.
Connect

We make warm, well-timed introductions to principals who genuinely fit. Not volume — quality access built on existing relationships.

IV.
Execute

We remain engaged through negotiation, due diligence, and close — ensuring the process moves efficiently toward a decisive outcome.

Principal & Founder, Dee & Co

Deeptesh Mejari is the founding principal of Dee & Co. His advisory practice is built around a core conviction: that the most valuable introductions are made quietly, the most consequential capital decisions require time, and the most durable partnerships are built on judgment rather than speed.

He works with a select group of founders and investors navigating private markets, growth capital, and strategic transitions — providing the kind of counsel that is rarely found within larger institutions. Every engagement is approached as a long-horizon relationship, not a transaction.
